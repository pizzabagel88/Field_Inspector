Field Inspector Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Field Inspector is provided by pizzabagel. This policy explains how the Android app uses data on your device.

Information the app uses

Field Inspector uses the camera to capture photos. If you enable GPS coordinates or elevation in the photo information settings, it requests your foreground location to display and embed those values in photos. It uses the device magnetometer to show a compass heading when available. You may enter a project name, inspector name, and custom note. The app also keeps your photo information layout and next photo sequence number on your device.

Storage and sharing

Captured photos, including any information you choose to embed, are saved to your device's Pictures/Field Inspector album. Settings remain on your device. Field Inspector does not operate an account, analytics service, advertising service, or server that receives your photos, location, or settings. The app does not sell your data. If you share photos or enable a device or gallery cloud-backup service, that service's terms and privacy practices apply; Field Inspector does not control those services.

Optional tips

The optional “Tip the developer” link opens PayPal.Me in your browser. Tipping does not unlock any app features or content. Field Inspector does not receive payment details from PayPal; if you choose to tip, PayPal handles that transaction under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Camera access is needed to take photos. Location access is optional and is used only while the app is open to show GPS coordinates or elevation. You can deny or revoke either permission in Android Settings. The app can still be used without location; the affected photo information will show as unavailable.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved photos using your device's gallery or file manager. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored settings, but photos saved in the shared Pictures album may remain until you delete them separately.

Children

Field Inspector is a general-purpose field documentation tool and is not designed specifically for children.
